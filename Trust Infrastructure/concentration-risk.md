# InfinityGrid Trust Infrastructure Concentration Risk

## When One Cybersecurity Service Becomes Everyone's Dependency

Cybersecurity concentration risk occurs when a large number of users, enterprises, government agencies, products, or security workflows depend upon a relatively small number of external systems.

The more valuable a service becomes, the more dependency it can accumulate.

That dependency creates a paradox:

> The success of a cybersecurity service can itself increase the consequences of its failure.

## Concentration Risk Is Not the Same as Security Risk

A platform may have excellent security controls while still representing high concentration risk.

Examples:

- strong encryption does not eliminate dependency
- good engineering does not eliminate continuity risk
- strong authentication does not eliminate acquisition risk
- reliable operations do not eliminate governance risk

InfinityGrid therefore evaluates concentration separately from ordinary technical security.
