# InfinityGrid Incident Intelligence Standard (IGIIS)

Version 1.0

The InfinityGrid Incident Intelligence Standard governs how InfinityGrid identifies, researches, classifies, verifies, publishes, updates, and corrects public cyber incident intelligence.

The objective is not to publish the fastest account of an incident.

The objective is to maintain the most defensible public record possible with the evidence available at the time.

## Core Principles

### Evidence Before Narrative

InfinityGrid does not determine what happened first and search for evidence afterward.

Evidence determines the narrative.

### Facts and Claims Are Separate

Statements from organizations, threat actors, researchers, regulators, journalists, litigants, and anonymous sources are not automatically treated as equivalent evidence.

Every material assertion should be classified according to the strength of the underlying evidence.

### Technical Unknowns Stay Unknown

InfinityGrid does not infer a CVE, attack vector, threat actor, malware family, compromised identity, control failure, or root cause simply because one explanation appears likely.

Where evidence does not establish the answer, the record states:

`Not publicly established.`

### Incidents Are Living Records

Cyber incidents evolve.

Affected populations change.

Technical findings emerge.

Regulators publish findings.

Litigation reveals new facts.

Companies revise disclosures.

InfinityGrid therefore treats every incident record as a versioned intelligence record rather than a one-time news article.

### Original Evidence Is Preferred

Where available, InfinityGrid prioritizes:

1. Regulatory and government records
2. Court records and sworn filings
3. SEC and equivalent securities disclosures
4. Organization breach notifications
5. Vendor security advisories
6. Primary technical research
7. High-quality independent reporting
8. Threat-actor statements and unverified claims

### Public Safety

InfinityGrid does not publish stolen personal information, credentials, private keys, exploit-ready victim infrastructure, or material that unnecessarily increases harm to affected individuals.

### Accountability Without Speculation

InfinityGrid may document failures, missed opportunities, inadequate controls, disclosure changes, and preventable conditions when supported by evidence.

InfinityGrid does not convert suspicion into fact.

## The InfinityGrid Intelligence Chain

Every incident may move through five analytical layers:

### BreachWatch
What is happening now?

### BreachLedger
What happened?

### SecurityReceipts
What is confirmed, claimed, disputed, or changed?

### CVE-to-Breach
Was a known vulnerability involved?

### Lessons
What technically failed, what could have detected it, and what could have broken the attack path?

Not every incident belongs in every layer.

Publication depends on the evidence available.

## Record Integrity

Every major InfinityGrid record should preserve:

- Incident identifier
- Organization
- Sector
- Geography
- Timeline
- Evidence status
- Technical findings
- Known unknowns
- Sources
- Last reviewed date
- Revision history

## Independence

Financial contributions, commercial relationships, sponsorships, partnerships, or requests from affected organizations do not determine whether an incident is included, removed, upgraded, downgraded, or corrected.

No pay-to-remove mechanism exists.
