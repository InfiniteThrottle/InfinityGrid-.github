# InfinityGrid Public Record Correction Protocol (IG-PRCP)

InfinityGrid treats corrections as part of intelligence quality control.

Corrections are not quietly erased.

## Material Corrections

A correction is material when it changes:

- affected population
- attack vector
- threat actor attribution
- CVE connection
- data categories
- incident dates
- affected organization
- technical root cause
- regulatory outcome
- severity
- evidence classification

## Correction Procedure

When a material error is identified:

1. Verify the new evidence.
2. Correct the affected record.
3. Preserve the Git history.
4. Update the record's last-reviewed date.
5. Add a correction note when the change materially alters the public understanding of the incident.
6. Link the evidence supporting the correction.

## Correction Format

### YYYY-MM-DD

**Previous record:**
1.4 million individuals affected.

**Corrected record:**
1.1 million individuals affected.

**Reason:**
A subsequently published regulatory filing established a more authoritative population count.

**Evidence level:**
E4 — Established

## Disputed Corrections

An organization requesting a correction must provide the specific statement being challenged and evidence supporting the requested change.

InfinityGrid does not remove accurate information solely because it is unfavorable.

## Removal

Removal may be appropriate when:

- the incident was incorrectly attributed,
- the underlying evidence was fabricated,
- publication creates unnecessary personal harm,
- content violates InfinityGrid's public-safety standards,
- legal requirements compel removal.

Removal is not available as a paid service.

## Silent Edits

Minor spelling, formatting, link, and grammatical corrections may be made without a formal correction record when they do not alter meaning.

## Researcher Accountability

InfinityGrid applies the same evidentiary standard to itself that it applies to organizations being documented.

If we are wrong, the public record should show that we corrected it.
